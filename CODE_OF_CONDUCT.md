# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and evidence-led. Critique methods and results rather than people. Welcome beginners and explain uncertainty without ridicule. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and deliberate publication of private information are not acceptable.

Keep public communication in English so international readers can participate. Share only material you have permission to share. Distinguish personal observations, reproducible tests and unverified hypotheses.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close off-topic threads and restrict repeated disruptive behaviour. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse; do not repeat private information in a public complaint. A dedicated private moderation contact remains to be approved by the owner.
