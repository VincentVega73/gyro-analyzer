# Roadmap

## Released

No production Android release yet. This repository provides development documentation and target resources.

## In development

1.0 focuses on trustworthy average RPM measurement, monochrome target acquisition, offline onboarding, four-language UI and reproducible validation.

## Planned before production

- Physical inkjet/laser target acceptance and multiple-device tests.
- Independent-reference accuracy and repeatability characterization.
- Approved legal identity, licensing and privacy contact.
- Owner-controlled production signing, Play Console testing and store listing.
- Reconciled screenshots, manuals and release notes for the accepted build.

## Under consideration

Wider device support and optional deeper rotation analysis. Wow & Flutter Lab is a possible research direction, not an announced feature or delivery commitment. No dates are promised.
