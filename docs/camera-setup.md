# Camera setup

1. Stop the platter before positioning paper. Keep the tonearm parked away from the target.
2. Attach the phone to a stable mount. Do not rest the phone on the rotating platter.
3. Frame the complete 180 mm disc. At 640 × 480, a disc width of about 180–400 pixels gives a marker width of about 12–27 pixels. Move closer if the marker is smaller than 12 pixels.
4. Prefer a near-overhead view. Moderate tilt can be handled by an affine ellipse; extreme perspective and lens distortion are not fully corrected.
5. Add diffuse light. Avoid glare, moving shadows, flickering lights and underexposure. The black mark must remain darker than its surrounding white paper.
6. Open Settings to select an available resolution and frame rate. Start with 640 × 480 and 30 FPS where available. 60 FPS may help sampling but can increase noise, heat and processing load. A requested rate is not proof of the delivered rate.
7. Inspect focus. Camera controls can request automatic or locked focus, exposure and white balance; capabilities depend on the device. The manual exposure preset is not guaranteed to be supported.
8. Recalibrate after any camera mode or position change. Keep the phone still through the measurement.

The preview uses the camera's displayed aspect ratio. Overlays and manual touches use the corresponding coordinate transform. A dashed predicted location is visual guidance, not an accepted observation. Handheld measurement is not supported as a reliable measurement mode.
