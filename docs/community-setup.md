# Community configuration

Required discussion categories: General; Questions & Support (Q&A); Measurement Results; Device Compatibility; Feature Ideas; Announcements (maintainer announcements).

The three prepared posts in `.github/DISCUSSION_TEMPLATE/` are publication drafts, not automatically installed category forms. Publish them to the appropriate categories and pin them after Discussions is enabled. Keep public titles and content in English. Issues use four structured forms for bugs, calibration, features and documentation.
