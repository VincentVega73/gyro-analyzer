# Draft: Gyro Analyzer 1.0

Status: unreleased; not a production announcement.

## Added

Offline monochrome target PDFs, automatic target acquisition, first-run preparation guide and EN/DE/ES/RU interface/help. Existing average-RPM methods, history and exports are retained.

## Known limitations

Physical target/printer/device acceptance and independent absolute-accuracy validation are pending. Camera modes vary by device. Strong perspective and handheld motion are unsupported. Calibration reuse is in memory only. No Google Play listing is published yet.

## Compatibility and documentation

Android 8 or later with a rear camera. See [compatibility](compatibility.md), [user guide](user-guide.md) and [accuracy](accuracy-and-uncertainty.md). Do not attach public APK/AAB assets without the owner's separate distribution decision.
